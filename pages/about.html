01 — Society Overview

The Manifesto.

Founded at the Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan, Jaipur, SKIT MUN CLUB is a premier diplomatic and parliamentary debating society. We train future leaders, engineers, and thinkers to navigate geopolitical complexities with analytical rigor and persuasive eloquence.

Our Mission

To cultivate critical analysis, multilateral diplomacy, and speechcraft across our student body through structured simulations and national circuit representation.

Academic Credibility

Mentored by distinguished faculty coordinators, integrating academic research methodologies with parliamentary rules of procedure.
