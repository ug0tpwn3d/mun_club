The Manifesto.

Founded at the Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan, Jaipur, SKIT MUN CLUB is a platform for finding your voice, developing your ideas, and learning to communicate them with confidence.

Public speaking is not about flawless English, complicated vocabulary, or sounding like a professional speaker from day one. It is about having something to say, understanding what you want to convey, and having the confidence to make others understand it.

We create an environment where students can debate, discuss, question, negotiate, present, and speak without the fear of being judged for their accent, grammar, or lack of experience. Through MUNs, debates, parliamentary simulations, open discussions, and speaking activities, we help students turn their thoughts into clear arguments and their hesitation into confidence.

Our goal extends beyond competitions. We want to build students who can stand in front of a classroom, an audience, an interview panel, a boardroom, or a stage and communicate their ideas effectively.

Our Mission

To build confident communicators.

We provide students with opportunities to speak, debate, discuss and present in an environment where ideas matter more than perfect English. Our focus is on developing clarity of thought, confidence, articulation, listening, argumentation, and the ability to communicate under pressure.

Beyond MUN

MUN is a medium. Communication is the goal.

While Model United Nations provides a powerful platform to develop these skills, the club is not limited to MUN. Debates, parliamentary discussions, public speaking, extempore, group discussions, negotiations, and open forums all serve the same purpose: helping students become better communicators.

Find Your Voice

You don't need perfect English to be a good speaker.

A strong speaker is not necessarily the person with the biggest vocabulary or the most polished accent. It is the person who can take an idea, structure it, and make another person understand it.

We want every student — experienced speaker or complete beginner — to have the confidence to step forward and speak.

A Letter from Faculty Coordination

The stage is temporary. The confidence stays.

The skills developed here are meant to extend far beyond college events. Whether it is a placement interview, a technical presentation, a team meeting, a classroom, a leadership role, or a room full of strangers, effective communication remains one of the most valuable skills a person can possess.

We are not just preparing students to speak at MUNs.
We are preparing them to be heard.

Mrs. Sanju Chaudhary & Dr. Shiv Priya · Faculty Coordinators
