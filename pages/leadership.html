From The Faculty Co-ordinators

On the discipline of
measured speech.

Dear Delegate,

When we first walked into a Model United Nations committee room, what struck us was not the size of the arguments but the quality of the listening. Diplomacy is not the art of winning a debate — it is the art of building a position so considered, so grounded in fact and empathy, that even your opposition pauses to recalibrate.

The theme, "आरंभ MUN" was built on a simple conviction: that every student from this institution — regardless of their branch, their semester, or their prior MUN experience — deserves a room where their voice carries weight. Not because they were born fluent in procedure, but because they chose to learn. That choice is already half the credential.

"The floor is not given to the loudest. It is earned by those who come prepared, speak with precision, and stay curious beyond their own clause."
— Dr. Shiv Priya, Faculty Co-ordinator

We have watched the SKIT MUN CLUB grow from an informal defence aspirants group of twelve students to a conference with 40+ delegates. What has not changed — and what we actively protect — is the culture of rigour inside the committee room. We do not award speaking time to titles. We award it to preparation.

To every delegate attending आरंभ MUN 2026: read your study guide twice. Source your claims. Disagree on record. And when the chair calls for a moderated caucus, do not stand at the podium to restate your bloc's position — stand there to move the room.

We look forward to watching you rise.

Dr. Shiv Priya & Mrs. Sanju Chaudhary

To everyone who registered for आरंभ MUN 2026,

यह सम्मेलन किसी बड़े मंच से नहीं, बल्कि एक कक्षा से शुरू हुआ था। इसकी तैयारी, अध्ययन सामग्री, समिति की योजना और हर छोटी-बड़ी व्यवस्था छात्रों ने मिलकर की है। हममें से कई लोग कभी उसी जगह बैठे थे, जहाँ आज आप बैठे हैं। उस समय हम भी पहली बार MUN के बारे में जान रहे थे और सोच रहे थे कि क्या हम इस मंच का हिस्सा बन सकते हैं।

आप बन सकते हैं। यही All India Political Party Meet - AIPPM का उद्देश्य है।.

AIPPM केवल भाषण देने या पुरस्कार जीतने की जगह नहीं है। यह अपनी बात रखना, दूसरों की बात समझना और सही सवाल पूछना सीखने का मंच है। यहाँ केवल यह नहीं देखा जाता कि कौन सबसे अच्छा बोलता है, बल्कि यह भी देखा जाता है कि कौन अपनी बात को समझकर रखता है और अपने विचारों पर ईमानदारी से सोचता है।

आत्मविश्वास से बोलना अच्छी बात है, लेकिन अपनी बात को सही तरीके से समझना और दूसरों के विचारों को सम्मान के साथ सुनना उससे भी ज़रूरी है।

All India Political Party Meet - AIPPM में आपकी आवाज़ मायने रखती है। यहाँ आपका उद्देश्य केवल जीतना नहीं, बल्कि एक बेहतर वक्ता और बेहतर विचारक बनना है।

“Never doubt your value. Your voice deserves to be heard, your ideas deserve to be shared, and your perspective has the power to make a difference. Speak with confidence, stand by your thoughts, and never be afraid to take your place in the room.”

We are proud of what this club has become. We are even more curious about what you will make of it on October 23rd.

Nishant Kumar, Keshav Saini, Shivansh Dwivedi & Ronak Dev

On the theme:
आरंभ.

The name we chose is not incidental. "आरंभ" — a Sanskrit word meaning beginning, initiation, the first act of a new order — captures exactly what we believe every committee session should feel like. Not a rehearsal of positions already held, but a genuine beginning of something the room did not expect.

The committee for 2026 — All India Political Party Meet (AIPPM) — was chosen because it represents different layers of domestic governance. A delegate who can understand the procedural texture of parliamentary debate is a delegate who understands why the two are inseparable.

We ask every delegate, regardless of experience level, to approach their committee with the same orientation: what does this room need to move forward, and how can my position help it get there? Awards follow preparation. Preparation follows curiosity.

The floor will open at 09:00 on October 23rd, 2026.

Order. Order. The delegate may speak.
