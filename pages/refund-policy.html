Refund & Cancellation Policy.

SKIT MODEL UNITED NATIONS (SKIT MUN CLUB) · Official Refund & Cancellation Policy · Last Updated: March 21, 2026 · Jaipur, India

SKIT Model United Nations (SKIT MUN CLUB) operates on a strict financial planning model. Because logistics, venues, conference kits, delegate materials, and adjudication resources are procured well in advance based on confirmed registration numbers, all financial transactions are final and serve as a genuine pre-estimate of damages incurred upon cancellation.

1. Strict No-Refund Policy

General Rule: All registration fees, accommodation charges, social event passes, and delegation fees are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.

Liquidated Damages: In accordance with the Indian Contract Act, 1872, any cancellation, withdrawal, or no-show by a Delegate—regardless of personal emergencies, medical issues, or academic/scheduling conflicts—will result in the total forfeiture of the fees paid, which shall be retained by SKIT MUN CLUB as reasonable liquidated damages to cover administrative and logistical sunk costs.

Disciplinary Expulsion: Delegates expelled for violating the Terms and Conditions or the Conference Code of Conduct forfeit their entire registration fee instantly with no recourse to any refund.

2. Event Postponement or Format Change

If ARAMBH MUN 2026 or any SKIT MUN conference is postponed, relocated, or shifted to a virtual/online format due to Force Majeure, administrative necessity, campus directives, or government regulations, no refunds will be granted. The Delegate's registration and fees will automatically be carried forward to the rescheduled dates or the virtual edition without penalty.

3. Event Cancellation

In the highly unlikely event that ARAMBH MUN 2026 is permanently canceled without being rescheduled or moved online, the Secretariat may, at its sole discretion, issue partial refunds. Such refunds will only be calculated after deducting all administrative, logistical, and preparatory costs already incurred by the club.

5. Policy Updates and Amendments

Right to Amend: The SKIT MUN CLUB Secretariat reserves the unconditional right to update, modify, or amend this Refund Policy at any time to reflect changing operational, legal, or financial requirements, without prior individual notice.

Agreement to Future Updates: By registering for the conference, the Delegate (and their legally binding parent/guardian) explicitly agrees to be bound by the most current version of this policy. It is the Delegate's responsibility to periodically review this document on our website/portal. Continued participation or failure to withdraw immediately upon an update constitutes absolute acceptance of all subsequent modifications.

For billing support or queries regarding financial reconciliation, contact the Treasury Desk at skitmg.mun.club@gmail.com with your Transaction ID, full name, and registered phone number.
