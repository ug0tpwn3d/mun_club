SKIT MUN Announces आरंभ MUN 3.0 2026 Core Agenda

The Secretariat of SKIT Model United Nations Club has formally announced the dates and committee roster for आरंभ MUN 2026. Featuring the All India Political Parties Meet (AIPPM) as its central parliamentary simulation, the conference brings together collegiate delegates for political debate, policy formulation, legislative negotiation, and high-pressure parliamentary discourse. Early-bird registrations are now open for collegiate delegations.
