06 — Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about preparing for our conferences, understanding procedural mechanics, and securing your delegation.

Model United Nations is an authentic academic simulation of the UN General Assembly, Security Council, and other multilateral bodies. Students assume the role of foreign diplomats to debate real-world issues, negotiate resolutions, and defend sovereign foreign policy.
Yes. Chambers such as UNGA-DISEC and Lok Sabha are curated specifically to provide a supportive arena for delegates attending their first conference. Furthermore, our Secretariat conducts pre-conference orientation workshops on speechcraft and procedural motions.
Delegates are expected to maintain formal diplomatic decorum. Western Business Formals (suits, blazers, formal shirts and trousers/skirts) are mandatory for UN committees. Indian Traditional Formals (Kurta-Pajama with Nehru jacket, Bandhgala, or formal Sarees) are permitted and encouraged in Indian legislative bodies such as the Lok Sabha.
Portfolios are allocated on a rolling, first-come-first-served basis based on the preferences chosen during registration and previous debate experience. Allocations and background guides are communicated via email prior to the conference date.
Submission of a Position Paper is mandatory for delegates competing for Best Delegate and High Commendation awards. It also serves as your core research outline during moderated caucuses.
Each committee awards Best Delegate, High Commendation, and a Special Mention. The Best Delegation award is given to the school/institution with the highest aggregate performance across all committees. Verbal commendations are issued at the Chairs' discretion during sessions.
ARAMBH MUN 2026 is held at the Kautaliya Seminar Hall, MBA Block, Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan (SKIT), Jaipur, Rajasthan on October 23rd, 2026. Registration opens at 08:30. Committees convene at 09:00.
