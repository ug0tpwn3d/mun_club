Conference Checklist & Prep Protocol.

By SKIT MUN Editorial Team · 7 Min Read

A successful delegate arrives at the conference venue with mental clarity and organized materials. Eliminate morning anxiety with this battle-tested preparation framework.

1. The 7-Day Pre-Conference Timeline

  • T-minus 7 Days: Finish your initial Position Paper draft; review it against official UN editorial style guides.
  • T-minus 4 Days: Rehearse your 90-second Opening Speech (GSL) with a timer. Eliminate vocal fillers ("um", "like", "you know").
  • T-minus 2 Days: Submit the Position Paper to the Executive Board for award consideration; prepare your digital and physical binder.
  • T-minus 1 Day: Pack your formal attire, inspect accessories (ties, blazers, badges), and double-check venue transportation.

2. What Belongs in the Physical Delegate Binder

While laptops and tablets are frequently permitted during unmoderated caucuses, many committees enforce digital blackouts during formal debate. Always have physical copies of:

  • Printed copy of the Committee Study Guide / Background Guide.
  • Printed copy of your submitted Position Paper.
  • Summary sheets of 3–5 previous UN resolutions on the agenda.
  • Your 1-page Country Policy Dossier and talking points.
  • Official conference Rules of Procedure (ROP) cheat sheet.
  • Blank legal pad, sticky notes (for sending chits), and black/blue pens.

3. Chit Etiquette & Executive Board Communication

Chits are small paper slips passed between delegates through Executive Board pages or logistics runners during committee. Use chits strategically:

  • Substantive Lobbying: "Delegate of Germany, our delegation supports your clause on maritime corridor safety. Would you be open to forming a unified working paper during the upcoming unmod?"
  • Clarification of Intent: "Delegate of Brazil, does your previous speech propose mandatory or voluntary carbon taxes?"
  • Chit to Chair: "Honorable Executive Board, is the committee entertaining motions for a 15-minute moderated caucus on humanitarian border access?"

4. Final Morning Checklist

  • ✓ Conference Delegate Pass & College Student ID card.
  • ✓ Formal Western Business Attire or Indian Formal Wear (as per chamber guidelines).
  • ✓ Laptop/tablet with offline-saved research files and charging adapter.
  • ✓ Water bottle, notepad, and emergency stationeries.
  • ✓ Placid, calm diplomatic mindset ready for collaborative multilateralism.
