Your First Conference.

Walking into your first committee chamber can be intimidating. This field guide breaks down the preparation, decorum, and execution required to make an impact on day one.

1. Pre-Conference Preparation Checklist

Success in committee is 80% preparation before gaveling in:

  • Read the Background Guide (BG): Understand the historical timeline, previous UN treaties, and the EB's stated focal areas.
  • Build a Physical Research Binder: Include the UN Charter, relevant treaty texts, your nation's bilateral agreements, and statistical data.
  • Draft Your 90-Second GSL Speech: State your country's overarching perspective and propose 2-3 specific policy pillars.

2. Demystifying Points and Motions

Do not let parliamentary procedure silence you. Remember three essential tools:

  • Point of Parliamentary Inquiry: Used whenever you are confused about the flow of debate or rules. Ask the Chair directly.
  • Point of Personal Privilege: Used if you cannot hear the speaker or the room temperature is disrupting debate.
  • Motion for a Moderated Caucus: Raise your placard when the floor opens to introduce a focused topic and time limit.

3. The Art of the First Speech

Keep your opening remarks structured: Hook (a provocative quote or geopolitical reality), Policy (your country's actions), and Call to Action (what you urge fellow member states to consider).
